Annual Homeschool Evaluation

$60
Can be paid with an FES-UA (Unique Abilities) scholarship

Florida requires an educational evaluation every year for every child in a home education program. This is that evaluation, done entirely online, wherever you are in Florida.

Approved Step Up For Students provider for annual evaluations.

What's included

  • I review your portfolio on my own time — your child does not need to be there
  • A short, relaxed conversation with your child, by phone or video, whichever you prefer
  • A signed evaluation letter, emailed to you within 5 business days, for you to file with your district

Required by Florida Statute 1002.41(1)(f). The conversation with your child is part of what the law requires, and it is usually the part children enjoy.

Add-ons

  • Each additional child — $25
  • 48-hour rush — $50
  • Individualized Learning Plan added on — $75 (normally $125)

Founding families

My first ten families pay $50 for their first child's evaluation, in exchange for an honest review afterward. Scholarship families included.

Leaving home education?

Whether your child is going back to school or moving to a PEP scholarship, Florida requires one last evaluation, filed with your notice of termination within 30 days. (Florida Statute 1002.41(1)(c)) Read more →

How it works

  1. You book and pay (or reserve funds through EMA if using a scholarship)
  2. You email me your portfolio
  3. I review it on my own time
  4. We have a short conversation with your child, by phone or video
  5. I email you the signed evaluation letter within 5 business days
  6. You file it with your district — I don't file it for you

Paying with a Step Up scholarship

FES-UA families can pay for this from their scholarship account instead of out of pocket, two ways:

Direct billing. Find Lantern Learning Lab under Find Providers in EMA and reserve the funds. Step Up pays me after the evaluation is delivered, so there's nothing to pay at booking.

Reimbursement. Pay when you book, and I'll send you a reimbursement-ready invoice from Lantern Learning Lab with everything Step Up asks for: your child's full name, the service and date, the amount, and my Florida Department of Education certification number.

My credentials are already approved in EMA, so you do not need to send them with your claim.
