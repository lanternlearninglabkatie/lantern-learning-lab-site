Choice Navigator service · FES-UA, PEP, FTC, FES-EO

This is a conversation with you, not an evaluation of your child. You don't need a diagnosis, and your child doesn't need to be on the call — I'm working from what you share with me.

This might be you if

  • Meltdowns or shutdowns during learning, or emotional reactions bigger than the moment calls for
  • Your child freezes before starting a task, or needs constant prompting to stay on it
  • Transitions are hard, plans changing throws everything off, or time and materials get lost along the way
  • Sensory overwhelm — noise, touch, movement, or needing to move their body to settle
  • You're worn out from being the thing that keeps them regulated

This fits all kinds of learners — ADHD, autism, anxiety, sensory differences, gifted or twice-exceptional kids — and plenty of neurotypical kids who are just struggling with the routine. No diagnosis needed either way.

What's included

  • One focused call where we talk through what's actually happening — not a test, just a conversation about your child
  • I figure out which kind of support actually fits: executive function (starting tasks, staying on track, managing time), regulation (meltdowns, sensory overwhelm, staying calm enough to work), or a full Individualized Learning Plan if what's really needed is a plan built around how your child learns, not just a quick fix — most parents can't tell which until we talk it through, and that's exactly what this session is for
  • A written plan built around whichever one fits, emailed within 2–3 business days: why this pattern is happening, specific strategies that match how your child's brain works, and what to try this week
  • A few light curriculum pointers if they're useful

Start small

You won't get a list of twenty things to do differently. The plan is built so you can pick one or two strategies to try this week — no pressure to overhaul everything at once.

How it works

  1. You book your session
  2. We talk through what's hard, what helps, and what you'd like easier first
  3. I figure out whether executive function support, regulation strategies, or a full Individualized Learning Plan fits best, and build your written plan around that
  4. You get it by email within 2–3 business days, ready to start using
