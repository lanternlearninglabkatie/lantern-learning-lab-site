What's included

  • A conversation about what you're currently using, what isn't working, and what matters most to your family
  • 2-3 specific curriculum recommendations matched to your child's learning needs, your budget, and your format or faith preferences
  • Why each one fits, and what to watch for before you buy
  • A written shortlist you can refer back to, not just a verbal recommendation

This isn't a replacement for the New Homeschooler Starter Package — that session covers curriculum too, alongside district paperwork and getting oriented as a new family. The Lantern List is for families who just want the curriculum piece, on its own.

How it works

  1. You book your session
  2. You fill out a short intake form so I can come prepared
  3. We talk through what's working, what isn't, and what matters most to your family
  4. I build your shortlist and send it by email within 2-3 business days

Paying with a Step Up scholarship

Approved Step Up For Students provider for Choice Navigator services.

FES-UA, PEP, FTC and FES-EO families can pay for this from their scholarship account instead of out of pocket, two ways:

Direct billing. Find Lantern Learning Lab under Find Providers in EMA and reserve the funds. Step Up pays me after the shortlist is delivered, so there's nothing to pay at booking.

Reimbursement. Pay when you book, and I'll send you a reimbursement-ready invoice from Lantern Learning Lab with everything Step Up asks for: your child's full name, the service and date, the amount, and my Florida Department of Education certification number.

My credentials are already approved in EMA, so you do not need to send them with your claim.
