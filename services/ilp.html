Choice Navigator service · FES-UA, PEP, FTC, FES-EO

There's no IEP in homeschool — once your child leaves public school enrollment, the IEP stops applying, and Florida doesn't require or provide any equivalent plan. This is modeled on the same idea, built for homeschool: not a legal document, no diagnosis required, just a written plan for how your specific child learns. Read more →

What's included

  • We talk through how your child learns best: what helps them focus, what trips them up, what keeps them engaged
  • A written plan: learning goals, strategies tailored to your child, and practical recommendations you can use right away
  • Built to work alongside whatever curriculum you're already using, or to help you choose one — you don't have to switch anything
  • Concrete, not theoretical: if a worksheet-based lesson isn't landing, the plan says what to try instead, not just why it's happening

How it works

  1. You book your plan
  2. We talk through how your child learns, where things get stuck, and what's already working
  3. I build your written plan from that conversation
  4. You get it by email, ready to use with whatever curriculum you have

Paying with a Step Up scholarship
