What a Choice Navigator does

Step Up For Students defines a Choice Navigator as consultations on "the selection of, application for, and enrollment in educational options ...; curriculum selection; and advice on career and postsecondary education opportunities." A navigator may not oversee or control a student's curriculum — so everything here is advice and options, with the choice always staying yours.

Paying with a Step Up scholarship

Approved Step Up For Students provider for Choice Navigator services.

FES-UA, PEP, FTC and FES-EO families can pay for this from their scholarship account instead of out of pocket, two ways:

Direct billing. Find Lantern Learning Lab under Find Providers in EMA and reserve the funds. Step Up pays me after the service is delivered, so there's nothing to pay at booking.

Reimbursement. Pay when you book, and I'll send you a reimbursement-ready invoice from Lantern Learning Lab with everything Step Up asks for: your child's full name, the service and date, the amount, and my Florida Department of Education certification number.

My credentials are already approved in EMA, so you do not need to send them with your claim.
