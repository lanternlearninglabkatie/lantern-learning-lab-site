About Lantern Learning Lab

I'm Katie Hursey. I help Florida homeschool families stay compliant and confident — the annual paperwork, the scholarship rules, and the everyday decisions — entirely online, wherever you are in the state.

Approved Step Up For Students provider for annual evaluations and Choice Navigator services.

Credentials

  • Florida Professional Teaching Certificate, K–6 (Active)
  • Prekindergarten Certification
  • ESOL Endorsement
  • ESE Endorsement
  • Gifted Endorsement

Because the law puts the requirement on the certificate, not on matching a student's grade, I can evaluate any grade level — elementary through high school.

Why the endorsements matter

Most evaluators hold a plain certificate. The ESE, Gifted and ESOL endorsements mean families with an IEP child, a twice-exceptional child, or an English language learner are working with someone who understands their child specifically, not just home education in general.

Services

Annual portfolio evaluations, Choice Navigator consultations for scholarship families, Individualized Learning Plans, executive function support, and behavior and regulation strategy sessions — all online.
