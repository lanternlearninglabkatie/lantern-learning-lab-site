About Lantern Learning Lab

I'm Katie Hursey. I help Florida homeschool families stay compliant and confident — the annual paperwork, the scholarship rules, and the everyday decisions — entirely online, wherever you are in the state.

Approved Step Up For Students provider for annual evaluations and Choice Navigator services.

My story

Lantern Learning Lab started in a special education classroom. I was looking around at fifteen students, each with their own individualized plan — thoughtful, intentional, built around exactly where that child was — and wondering how I could possibly deliver that level of attention to all fifteen at once. Traditional classrooms, even with the best intentions, aren't built for real personalization. Homeschooling is. I left the classroom to work with families directly, without a system that can't bend far enough to meet every child where they are.

Alongside this work, I also teach science hands-on at a nonprofit, building learning experiences for kids of all ages — the same hands-on approach behind Lantern's own science packs.

Credentials

  • Florida Professional Teaching Certificate: Elementary Education, Grades K–6
  • Florida Professional Certification: Prekindergarten/Primary Education, Age 3 through Grade 3
  • Florida Professional ESOL Endorsement
  • Bachelor of Science in Education, University of Tampa — Cum Laude, Honors Distinction, with coursework in exceptional student education (ESE) and gifted education
  • 14 years in education

Because the law puts the requirement on the certificate, not on matching a student's grade, I can evaluate any grade level — elementary through high school.

Why my background matters

Most evaluators hold a plain certificate. My degree coursework in exceptional student education and gifted education, plus my state ESOL endorsement, mean families whose child had an IEP at school, a twice-exceptional child, or an English language learner are working with someone who understands their child specifically, not just home education in general. There's no IEP in homeschool — the supports don't carry over just because the paperwork did — which is exactly the gap I help families fill.

What I bring

State-certified evaluator

Calm, supportive evaluations, experienced in documenting authentic learning and growth — built to help families feel confident and prepared, not judged.

Learning needs specialist

Familiar with a wide range of learning styles and curricula, so recommendations fit your family's actual rhythm, not a one-size-fits-all plan.

Neurodiverse advocacy

Educational foundation

A background in child development and teaching principles, with ongoing professional development to stay current.

Services
